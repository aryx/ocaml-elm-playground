Welcome to TinyNetscape

The page Netscape Navigator opened on, December 1994: its extensions to HTML, all at once

Netscape did not wait for anyone to agree: its first version drew pages in colour, text in bigger and smaller sizes, <CENTER>, rules as thick and as wide as a page asked, and pictures the text flows around, like the one on the left. Mosaic reads the same page and shows the words, not the looks: every tag it does not know is a tag it ignores.

A picture can float on the right too, and a paragraph that ends before it does leaves the next one to flow around what is left of it.
TinyMosaic's page is below both pictures: <BR CLEAR=ALL> moved it there.

What to try

Type a URL into the Location field; press Return. Try a short history of the web, or the first web site. Press s for this page's source: the tags Netscape invented are all there, in capitals, as 1995 wrote them.
