A short history of the web

The browsers TinyMosaic follows, in the order they came:

1990, WorldWideWeb
Tim Berners-Lee's first browser, at CERN, on a NeXT computer. It was an editor too: a page could be changed in place, and a link made by pointing at another page.
1991, the Line Mode Browser
Nicola Pellow's browser for any terminal: the page as text, and a number after each link. Press l to see this page as it showed it.
1993, NCSA Mosaic
Marc Andreessen and Eric Bina's browser: pictures in the page, among the words, and one click to follow a link.
1994, Netscape Navigator
Made by much of Mosaic's team: the page shown while it loads.

The first web page is still served by CERN, and the Line Mode Browser can be tried again in a simulator.
